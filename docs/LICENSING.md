Contributions accepted without a separate agreement are provided under the same
license.

The MIT license does not automatically grant rights to independently licensed
materials:

- Market data, sample datasets, broker APIs, and provider responses remain
  subject to their vendors' terms, attribution requirements, and redistribution
  restrictions.
- Third-party Python, JavaScript, and container dependencies retain their own
  licenses.
- Trademarks and service names belong to their respective owners.

Before publishing a release or container image, review bundled data and assets,
generate dependency license notices where required, and confirm that model
weights are not redistributed unless their terms allow it. Referencing or
downloading an artifact at runtime is not a representation that it shares this
repository's MIT license.

This document clarifies project intent and is not legal advice.
