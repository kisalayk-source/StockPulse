# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, give constructive feedback, accept responsibility for mistakes,
and focus discussion on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults,
threats, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and other
conduct inappropriate in a professional setting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact path in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Project maintainers may edit or remove
contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior
they consider inappropriate. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
